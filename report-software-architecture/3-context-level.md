## Context Level

### Audacity Users

The main categories (not mutually exclusive) of users that use Audacity are:

- **Simple Audio Editors** (70%): perform quick recordings, convert file formats, and apply basic edits.
- **Musicians** (60%): musical tasks, such as recording live music or digitizing vinyl records.
- **Audio Producers** (30%): production work, including mixing, mastering, and polishing audio with various effects.
- **Academic Users** (5%): academic purposes, such as audio and data analysis or developing Nyquist plugins.

Audacity is also used for smaller use cases, such as **adding metadata** or **creating subtitles**.
